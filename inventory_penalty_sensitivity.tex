\subsection{Inventory-Penalty Sensitivity}
\label{subsec:inventory-penalty-sensitivity}

The running inventory penalty $\phi$ was increased from $0.01$ to $0.10$ and the terminal inventory penalty $\alpha$ was increased from $0.001$ to $0.010$, a uniform tenfold increase of both parameters that preserves their original relative weighting. All three PPO architectures (Hamilton belief-state MLP, raw-return MLP, raw-return LSTM) were retrained from scratch under this higher-penalty reward, and the regime-conditioned analytical policy, the belief-weighted analytical policy, and the frozen supervised clone were all recomputed against the higher-penalty calibration.

Every other environment parameter (fill-decay $\kappa$, arrival intensity $\lambda$, adverse-selection jump size $\epsilon$, regime volatilities, transition generator, terminal time), every PPO/RecurrentPPO hyperparameter (learning rate, rollout length, mini-batch size, number of epochs, discount factor, GAE parameter, clipping range, initial policy standard deviation), the network architecture, the training budget ($1{,}000{,}000$ observable-event transitions per run), the number of learner seeds (5 per architecture), the training-environment seed, and the 500-path holdout evaluation set (identical paths/seeds to the original calibration) were held fixed between the two calibrations.
